## 共通プロンプト

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single game sprite original for a 30x30 roguelike tile. Create exactly ONE isolated object or creature, centered in a square transparent image. Saturated SNES pixel art matching a Japanese sea creature roguelike: chunky readable silhouette, dark brown almost black outline, 3-4 flat shading bands, light from upper left, large simple details readable when reduced to 26 pixels. No text, watermark, ground, shadow outside silhouette, background, framing, or extra objects. Leave generous transparent padding. Creatures full body three-quarter frontal view unless stated otherwise, clear eyes. Subject:
